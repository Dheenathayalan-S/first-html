About the College

J.J. College of Engineering and Technology is one of the premier engineering institutions in Tamil Nadu. The college offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in Engineering, Technology and Management Studies. The institution provides excellent infrastructure, experienced faculty members and modern laboratory facilities to enhance student learning.
